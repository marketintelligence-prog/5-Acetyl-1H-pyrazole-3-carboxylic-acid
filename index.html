01

Product Overview & Identity

5-Acetyl-1H-pyrazole-3-carboxylic acid (tautomer: 3-acetyl-1H-pyrazole-5-carboxylic acid) is a small, bifunctional pyrazole bearing a methyl ketone and a carboxylic acid. Its commercial importance comes almost entirely from one drug: it is the acid half of darolutamide. In the Orion/Bayer process the acid is amide-coupled (T3P/DIPEA) with (S)-4-(1-(2-aminopropyl)-1H-pyrazol-3-yl)-2-chlorobenzonitrile, and the acetyl group is then reduced with NaBH4 to the 1-hydroxyethyl group of darolutamide US 10,189,789. Suppliers label it "ODM-201 intermediate 1" (ODM-201 = darolutamide) ChemicalBook. It is also sold as "Darolutamide Impurity 10" reference standard for ANDA analytical work SynZeal.

Synonyms: 3-Acetyl-1H-pyrazole-5-carboxylic acid; 1H-Pyrazole-3-carboxylic acid, 5-acetyl-; 5-Acetylpyrazole-3-carboxylic acid; ODM-201 int1; Darolutamide Impurity 10.

Industry Applications, End-Products & Market Sizes

A. Pharmaceutical intermediate — darolutamide (≈95%+ of demand, MI estimate)

Darolutamide is an oral androgen-receptor inhibitor for nmCRPC and mHSPC (with or without docetaxel). The acid is the KSM that supplies the 5-(1-hydroxyethyl)pyrazole-3-carboxamide half of the molecule US 12,611,397.

B. Analytical reference standards

Sold as "Darolutamide Impurity 10" with full characterisation for ANDA method development, validation and QC SynZeal; Cato offers a full darolutamide impurity set Cato. Tiny volumes (grams), very high price per gram.

C. Research building block (medicinal chemistry)

Orion's androgen-receptor patents used the same acid to make several analogues (e.g. R-isomers) by the same coupling/reduction sequence US 9,657,003. The Hungarian process group also describes it as a versatile building block Synthesis 2023. Kilo-scale at most.

Other clusters

Specialty polymers, electronics/photoresist, agrochemicals, flavours/fragrances, cosmetics, industrial uses: no documented commercial use found. Pyrazole carboxylic acids are used in agrochemicals generally, but no product built on this specific acid was identified.

  • Simple chemistry from ester (1 step) — fast to qualify
  • Sourcing network in China already used for other KSMs
  • CRO capability for impurity standards

Weaknesses

  • No DMF-grade track record on this molecule
  • Small volumes (MT, not hundreds of MT)

Threats

  • Single-drug dependency
